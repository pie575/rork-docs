These are the most common general questions about Rork and how it works.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Rork work?">
    Describe your app idea in plain English, and Rork generates a working app: a native iPhone app in Swift, a native Android app in Kotlin, or a web app in React. You test it right in the browser, in the iOS simulator, or the Android emulator. The browser preview is a great way to see how your app feels quickly, but the details may vary and not all native features are available in the browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need coding experience to make my app?">
    No. Rork makes apps from prompts. Anyone with a vision can simply describe what they want to see and bring it to life. If you want to dive into the code, you can use Dev mode to edit directly, but it is never required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Rork for professional purposes?">
    Yes. Rork is used for a wide range of professional apps, including radio show companion apps, hospital management tools, and consumer businesses. Apps built with Rork support anywhere from 0 to 100,000 users.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What kinds of apps can I build?">
    iPhone (iOS) apps, Android apps, and web apps. Rork builds native apps: real Swift code for iPhone, real Kotlin code for Android, and React for web. You can have all three in the same project.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Rork good for production apps?">
    Yes. Rork supports apps in production with real users. Apps built with Rork have been published to the App Store and Google Play and are live in the hands of paying customers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
