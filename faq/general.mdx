General FAQ questions for Rork

## How does Rork work?

Describe your app idea in plain English, and Rork generates a working app: a native iPhone app in Swift, a native Android app in Kotlin, or a web app in React. You test it right in the browser, in the iOS simulator or the Android emulator, and you can install an iPhone app on your own device. The browser preview is a great way to see how your app feels quickly, but the details may vary, and not all native features are available in the browser.

### Do I need coding experience to make my app?

Rork makes apps in minutes just from prompts. Anyone with a vision can simply describe what they want to see and bring it to life.

### Can I use Rork for professional purposes?

Yes, you can use Rork for professional purposes. We have users creating apps for Radio shows, Hospital management, and building consumer businesses from their apps.
