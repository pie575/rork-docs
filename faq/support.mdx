These are the most common questions about getting help with Rork.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do you have priority support?">
    Rork Max users get priority chat support. Rork Pro users get email support. On the Free plan there is no included support channel, but you can always write to [support@rork.com](mailto:support@rork.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rork broke my app. What should I do?">
    Go back a version. **Undo** sits under the last answer, and **Revert** sits under every earlier answer, so you can return to any point in the chat. Going back costs no credits. See [Undo a change and go back a version](/features/undo-and-history).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rork got in an error loop. What should I do?">
    Stop adding messages on top of broken work. Revert to the last answer that worked, then ask for the change again in smaller steps. See [How to get out of an agent error loop](/introduction/introduction/agent-loops).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Email [support@rork.com](mailto:support@rork.com) or join the [Rork Discord](https://discord.gg/rxBHytVKaT). Rork Max users get priority chat support accessible from the dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is the community?">
    Join the [Rork Discord](https://discord.gg/rxBHytVKaT). Builders share tips, swap beta testers, and help each other with distribution strategies. It is the fastest place to get answers from real builders.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
