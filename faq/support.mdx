Frequently Asked Questions about support with Rork

### Do you have priority support?

**Rork Max** users get priority chat support.\
**Rork Pro** users get email support.\
On the **Free** plan there is no included support channel, but you can still write to [support@rork.com](mailto:support@rork.com).

### Rork broke my app. What should I do?

Go back a version. **Undo** sits under the last answer, and **Revert** sits under every earlier answer, so you can return to any point in the chat. Going back costs no credits.\
See [Undo a change and go back a version](/features/undo-and-history).

### Rork got in an error loop, what should I do?

Stop adding messages on top of broken work. Revert to the last answer that worked, then ask for the change again in smaller steps.\
See [How to get out of an error loop](/introduction/introduction/agent-loops).
