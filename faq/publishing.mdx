Frequently Asked Questions about publishing with Rork

## Publishing

Not sure which route to take? Start with the [publishing overview](/introduction/deploy-your-app).

### How can I share my app before it is in a store?

Publish the web app in your project and share its `.rork.app` link. Anyone can open it in a browser, with no App Store, no TestFlight, and no install. See [Rork Web](/web/rork-web).\
For an iPhone app, send testers a TestFlight link after your first submission. See [TestFlight and testers](/store-guides/testflight-beta-testers).

### How can I publish to App Store or TestFlight?

Use Rork’s built-in publishing feature: **Publish → App Store → Submit to App Store**.\
You’ll need an **Apple Developer Account**. Rork builds and uploads the app on its own machines, so no Mac, Xcode, or extra build account is needed.\
You finish the store listing (product page, pricing, screenshots) in App Store Connect.

### How can I publish to Google Play or download an .apk file?

Open **Publish → Google Play → Upload to Google Play**. Rork builds an Android App Bundle (AAB), signs it with a stable upload key, and can send it to your internal testing track.\
You create the app record in Google Play Console yourself, and you send releases for review there.\
The same build gives you an **APK download** when you want to install the app on a phone by hand.

### How can I publish to the web?

One click: **Publish → Publish to web**. Rork hosts the app at `your-app.rork.app`, and you can change the subdomain in the same panel. After the first publish you can buy a domain or connect one you own. See [Domains](/web/rork-web#domains).
