These are the most common questions about publishing and distributing Rork apps.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How can I share my app before it is in a store?">
    Publish the web app in your project and share its `.rork.app` link. Anyone can open it in a browser with no App Store, no TestFlight, and no install. See [Rork Web](/web/rork-web).

    For an iPhone app, send testers a TestFlight link after your first submission. See [TestFlight and testers](/store-guides/testflight-beta-testers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I publish to the App Store or TestFlight?">
    Use Rork's built-in publishing feature: **Publish → App Store → Submit to App Store**. You need an Apple Developer Account (\$99/year). Rork builds and uploads the app on its own machines, so no Mac, Xcode, or extra build account is needed. You finish the store listing (product page, pricing, screenshots) in App Store Connect. See [Publish to the App Store](/store-guides/publish-app-store).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I publish to Google Play or download an APK file?">
    Open **Publish → Google Play → Upload to Google Play**. Rork builds an Android App Bundle (AAB), signs it with a stable upload key, and can send it to your internal testing track. You create the app record in Google Play Console yourself and send releases for review there. The same build also gives you an APK download to install on your own phone. See [Publish to Google Play](/submitting-to-play-store/creating-the-aab-file).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I publish to the web?">
    One click: **Publish → Publish to web**. Rork hosts the app at `your-app.rork.app`, and you can change the subdomain in the same panel. After the first publish you can buy a domain or connect one you own. See [Rork Web](/web/rork-web).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a Mac to publish an iPhone app?">
    No. Rork builds and uploads your app on its own cloud machines. You do not need a Mac, Xcode, or any local build environment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My build succeeded but the app is not in App Store Connect. What should I do?">
    Open the App Store tab in your project. Every submission is listed there with its state and logs. Common causes: build still running (wait for it), a capability needing Apple approval, or a version already in review. See [Submission errors](/submitting-to-app-store/apple/submission-errors).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
