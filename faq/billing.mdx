These are the most common questions about Rork plans, credits, and billing.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does it cost?">
    Rork has a **Free** plan for designing your app, **Rork Pro** at $$20/month** (100 credits), and **Rork Max** from **$$**200/month** (1,000 credits and up). In India there is also **Rork Start** at **₹649/month** (40 credits). See [Plans and Subscriptions](/introduction/subscriptions) for what each plan includes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I hit my credit limit. What should I do?">
    Upgrade to a higher plan, or wait for your monthly reset. Paid plans reset on the monthly anniversary of your first purchase. The Free plan resets at the start of each calendar month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between build credits and Cloud credits?">
    Build credits are spent while you create and edit your app inside Rork (chat messages, builds, asset generation). Cloud credits are a separate USD balance that your finished app spends at runtime for AI features (chat, image generation, voice, web search). Two separate balances, billed independently. See [Build credits vs Cloud credits](/billing/build-credits-vs-cloud-credits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do my credits reset?">
    Paid plans (Rork Pro and Rork Max) reset every month on the anniversary of your first purchase. The Free plan resets at the start of each calendar month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get free credits?">
    Yes. Connect your RevenueCat account at [rork.com/revenuecat-gift](https://rork.com/revenuecat-gift) to earn up to 1,000 free credits based on your app revenue. Active members of the [Rork Discord](https://discord.gg/rxBHytVKaT) also earn credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade or downgrade my subscription?">
    On desktop, click your profile in the top right. If you are on the Free plan, click **Upgrade Plan**. If you already have a plan, click **Manage Subscription**. On mobile, manage your subscription through the App Store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel?">
    On desktop, open **Account Settings → Manage Subscription** from your profile menu. You will be taken to the Stripe billing portal to manage or cancel. On mobile, cancel through the App Store.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
