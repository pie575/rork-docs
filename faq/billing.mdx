Frequently Asked Questions about billing with Rork

## Billing

### How much does it cost?

Rork has a **Free** plan for designing your app, **Rork Pro** at **\$20/month** (100 credits), and **Rork Max** from **\$200/month** (1,000 credits and up). In India there is also **Rork Start** at **₹649/month** (40 credits).\
See [Plans & Subscriptions](/introduction/subscriptions) for what each plan includes.

### I hit my credit limit. What should I do?

Upgrade to a higher plan, or wait for your monthly reset.\
**Paid plans reset on the monthly anniversary of your first purchase.** The **Free** plan resets at the start of each calendar month.
