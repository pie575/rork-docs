Frequently Asked Questions about collaboration with Rork

## Collaboration

### Can I collaborate with others?

Yes. Press the invite button in the project header, add your teammate's email, and pick **Editor** or **Viewer**. You can also create an invite link instead.

Full guide: [Share a project and work together](/collaborate/share-a-project).

### Who pays for a collaborator's messages?

The project owner. Everything an editor sends comes out of the owner's credits.
