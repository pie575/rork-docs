These are the most common questions about sharing and collaborating on Rork projects.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I collaborate with others?">
    Yes. Press the invite button in the project header, add your teammate's email, and pick **Editor** or **Viewer**. You can also create an invite link instead. Full guide: [Share a project and collaborate](/collaborate/share-a-project).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Editor and Viewer?">
    An Editor can send messages to the agent, edit code and secrets, publish, and invite other people. A Viewer can read the chat and the preview, and leave comments, but cannot edit or publish. Give clients and stakeholders Viewer, and give teammates Editor.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who pays for a collaborator's messages?">
    The project owner. Everything an editor sends comes out of the owner's credits. See [Build credits vs Cloud credits](/billing/build-credits-vs-cloud-credits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can two people work at the same time?">
    Both can be in the project simultaneously, but only one message runs at a time. Agree who is driving before you both start typing, or take turns.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a collaborator need their own paid plan?">
    No. They need a Rork account. The work runs on the project owner's credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I stop someone from having access?">
    Open the invite dialog and press **Remove** on their row. An invite link you no longer want can be deleted the same way.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
