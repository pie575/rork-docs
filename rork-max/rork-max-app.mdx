## What you can do with the Rork Max app

### Build apps from your phone

The complete Rork Max chat, redesigned for iPhone: plans, todos, questions, and build progress. Start a new app or open any app you've already built and continue whenever you want. All your apps, sessions, and their latest state are waiting right there, in the **Apps** tab. Each project shows whether it is **Swift**, **Web**, or both.

The chat works the same way as Rork Max on the web:

- **Voice input**: dictate your message instead of typing, then send it or edit it first.
- **Images and annotations**: attach photos, or grab a screenshot of the running app right from the chat and draw on it to point at exactly what you want changed.
- **File uploads**: attach documents straight from the Files app (specs, CSVs, designs) so the agent can work with them.
- **Plan mode**: have the agent propose a plan and review it before any code is written.
- **Model picker**: choose the AI model for each message, or leave it on Auto.
- **Message queue**: while the agent is working, keep sending follow-ups. They line up above the composer and run one after another. Tap a queued message to edit it, or tap **X** to remove it before it starts.
- **Agent widgets**: the agent can ask you questions, request API keys, generate images, sounds, and 3D models for your app, and show live build progress, all inside the chat.

### Preview natively, no TestFlight needed

Swift builds run natively inside the app. Full speed, real gestures, no streaming, no simulator. You're testing the real thing, not a video feed of it.

While your Swift app is running, a floating orange Rork button stays on top of it (drag it anywhere if it's in the way):

- **Tap** it to open the chat and keep iterating on your app.
- **Press and hold** it to leave the running app and get back to your list of apps.

### Publish to the App Store from your phone

Open **More → Publish App** to reach **App Store Submissions**, or simply ask the agent in chat to publish your app. From the submissions hub you can start a new submission, track uploads, open TestFlight, and share a **Beta Invite Link** with testers.

## How to install the Rork Max app

Rork Max runs the apps you build as real native code inside itself, which is why it can't live on the App Store. Instead, you install it once from your browser. It takes about five minutes, and there's nothing to download on your computer.

Everything happens on one page: go to **[rork.com/ios/install](https://rork.com/ios/install?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=rork-max-app&utm_content=install-section)** and follow the steps there.

You only need the computer once. After that, everything lives on your iPhone: Rork Max updates itself over the air, so new features simply arrive the next time you open the app. No cable, no reinstalling.

Occasionally we ship a new version of the Rork Max app itself that can't arrive over the air. In that case, just go back to [rork.com/ios/install](https://rork.com/ios/install?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=rork-max-app&utm_content=install-section-reinstall) and run the install again. It always installs the latest version and replaces the one on your phone.

## Requirements

- An iPhone running **iOS 18 or later** (check in Settings → General → About)
- A **Mac with Chrome or Edge** for the one-time install (Windows isn't supported yet)
- A **data USB cable**, preferably the one that came with your iPhone or any MFi-certified cable (charge-only cables won't work)
- A free or paid **Apple Developer account**. A free account lets you keep up to 3 sideloaded apps installed at a time; a paid membership removes that limit
- A **paid Rork plan** to build Swift apps. **Rork Pro** (\$20/month) already covers native iPhone apps: see [pricing](https://rork.com/pricing?utm_source=docs&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=rork-max-app&utm_content=requirements-pricing)

<Info>
  On Chrome or Edge on a Mac, you don't need the Rork Companion desktop app anymore. Everything from installing Rork Max to putting your apps on your iPhone happens in the browser and inside the app itself. Companion sticks around for browsers the installer doesn't support yet: see [testing with the Companion app](/rork-max-swift).
</Info>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which subscription do I need to use the Rork Max app?">
    You need a paid plan to build apps. **Rork Pro** ($20/month) covers native iPhone apps with SwiftUI, so it is enough for the app. **Rork Max** (from $200/month) adds iPad, Apple Watch, Vision Pro, native games, and iOS widgets: see [pricing](https://rork.com/pricing?utm_source=docs\&utm_medium=referral\&utm_campaign=rork-max-app\&utm_content=faq-pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it work only on iPhone?">
    Yes, Rork Max is an iPhone app. It needs iOS 18 or later. You can check your version in Settings → General → About.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a Mac or a Windows PC?">
    You'll need a Mac for the initial install. Open the installer in Chrome or Edge (Safari and Firefox can't talk to iPhones over USB). Windows isn't supported yet. The good news: you only need the computer once, and after that everything lives on your iPhone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to install anything on my computer?">
    Nope. There's nothing to download, the whole installer runs in your browser. Just plug in your iPhone with a USB cable and follow the steps on the [install page](https://rork.com/ios/install?utm_source=docs\&utm_medium=referral\&utm_campaign=rork-max-app\&utm_content=faq-install).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is it safe to sign in with my Apple Account?">
    Yes. Your Apple credentials, certificates, and pairing data stay in your browser, and app signing happens locally on your machine. Nothing is sent to Rork's servers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid Apple Developer account?">
    No, both free and paid Apple Developer accounts work. The only difference: a free account lets you keep up to 3 sideloaded apps installed at a time, while a paid membership removes that limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need an original cable?">
    Preferably, yes: the one that came with your iPhone, or any MFi-certified cable. It must be a data cable, not a charge-only one. With uncertified cables the connection can drop mid-install, and we can't guarantee it will work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happened to Rork Companion?">
    If you're on Chrome or Edge on a Mac, you don't need Companion anymore. Everything from installing Rork Max to putting your apps on your iPhone now happens in the browser and inside the app itself. Companion sticks around for browsers the installer doesn't support yet: see [testing with the Companion app](/rork-max-swift).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I really install full apps to my iPhone from Rork Max?">
    Yes, really. The apps you build land on your Home Screen as real iOS apps. You can launch them like anything else and test widgets, camera, Dynamic Island, and other native features right away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I build Web apps in the Rork Max app?">
    Yes. When you tap **+**, pick **Web** as the platform (or open an existing Web project from the Apps tab). Web apps preview inside Rork Max and can open in the browser from Project Actions. Native Apple features like widgets and Dynamic Island still need a Swift app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can it run only Swift apps?">
    No. Rork Max supports **Swift** and **Web** projects. Swift unlocks native Apple features: widgets, Live Activities, ARKit, HealthKit, even 3D games. Not sure what to pick? See [iPhone, Android, or web: what's the difference](/swift-vs-react-native/whats-the-difference).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I publish to the App Store from my phone?">
    Yes for Swift apps. Open **More → Publish App**, then use **App Store Submissions** for uploads, TestFlight, and beta invite links. You can also just ask the agent in chat to publish your app, and it will walk you through the submission. Web apps are not published to the App Store from this flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do updates work?">
    Rork Max updates itself over the air for most releases. Install it once, and new features simply arrive the next time you open the app. Occasionally a host update needs another pass through [rork.com/ios/install](https://rork.com/ios/install?utm_source=docs\&utm_medium=referral\&utm_campaign=rork-max-app\&utm_content=faq-updates); that install replaces the app on your phone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I see multiple Rork Max apps on my iPhone?">
    Each install is tied to the Apple Developer team you signed with, so if you've installed Rork Max with different Apple accounts or teams, each one shows up as a separate app. Reinstalling with the same Rork account and the same Apple team always replaces the existing app instead of adding a new one. Feel free to delete the copies you don't use, just keep in mind that a free Apple Developer account only allows 3 sideloaded apps at a time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving the SMS verification code from Apple">
    Instead of waiting for the SMS, grab the code from a trusted device: on any iPhone, iPad, or Mac where you're already signed in, go to **Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Get Verification Code**. Apple's [verification code guide](https://support.apple.com/102606) walks through every option. Still stuck? Reach [Apple Support for Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/apple-account). SMS delivery is on their side.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app won't launch after installing">
    Two things to check. First, trust the developer on your iPhone: **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management** → tap your account → **Trust**. Second, make sure Developer Mode is on: **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode**. And if you're on a free Apple Developer account, Apple allows only 3 sideloaded apps at a time, so you may have hit that limit. The paid [Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/) (\$99/year) removes it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I use it after installation?">
    Just open Rork Max whenever inspiration strikes. All your apps, sessions, and their latest state are waiting right there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
