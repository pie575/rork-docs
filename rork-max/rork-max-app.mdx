Rork Max is the companion iPhone app that runs the native iOS apps you build with Rork right on your iPhone, replacing Xcode and TestFlight without compromising performance. You can build Swift and Web apps, install them to your Home Screen, and publish to the App Store, all from your phone.

<Info>
  The Rork Max app shows native **Swift** apps and **Web** apps. Android (Kotlin) projects do not appear in the Apps tab.

  If you sign in and the list is empty, you probably have no Swift or Web project yet. Start one at [rork.com](https://rork.com) or tap **\+** in the app and choose **Swift** or **Web**. Building Swift apps needs a paid plan: **Rork Pro** (\$20/month) is enough. See [Plans and Subscriptions](/introduction/subscriptions).
</Info>

## What you can do with the Rork Max app

### Build apps from your phone

The complete Rork chat is available on iPhone: plans, todos, questions, and build progress. Start a new app or open any app you have already built and continue whenever you want. All your apps and sessions are in the **Apps** tab.

Tap **\+** and describe what you want to build. Choose **Swift** or **Web** as the platform before you create. The chat works the same way as Rork on the web, with voice input, image attachments, plan mode, model picker, and a message queue for sending follow-ups while the agent works.

### Preview natively, no TestFlight needed

Swift builds run natively inside the app. Full speed, real gestures, no streaming, no simulator. You are testing the real thing.

While your Swift app is running, a floating orange Rork button stays on top of it. Tap it to open the chat and keep iterating. Press and hold it to leave the running app and get back to your list.

### Install apps to your Home Screen

Rork Max can install the apps you build directly to your device. They land on your Home Screen as real iOS apps and let you test native iOS features:

- Widgets
- Camera
- Dynamic Island and Live Activities
- Screen blocking and focus controls

### Publish to the App Store from your phone

Open **More → Publish App** to reach **App Store Submissions**, or ask the agent in chat to publish your app. From there you can start a new submission, track uploads, open TestFlight, and share a **Beta Invite Link** with testers.

## How to install the Rork Max app

Rork Max runs the apps you build as real native code inside itself, which is why it cannot live on the App Store. Instead, you install it once from your browser.

Go to [**rork.com/ios/install**](https://rork.com/ios/install) and follow the steps there. You only need the computer once. After that, Rork Max updates itself over the air so new features simply arrive the next time you open the app.

## Requirements

- An iPhone running **iOS 18 or later**
- A **Mac with Chrome or Edge** for the one-time install (Windows is not supported yet)
- A **data USB cable** (charge-only cables will not work)
- A free or paid **Apple Developer account** (free allows up to 3 sideloaded apps; paid removes that limit)
- A **paid Rork plan** to build Swift apps. Rork Pro (\$20/month) is enough.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which subscription do I need?">
    You need a paid plan to build apps. Rork Pro ($$20/month) covers native iPhone apps with SwiftUI. Rork Max (from $$200/month) adds iPad, Apple Watch, Vision Pro, native games, and iOS widgets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a Mac or a Windows PC?">
    You need a Mac for the initial install, using Chrome or Edge. Windows is not supported yet. You only need the computer once.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid Apple Developer account?">
    No, both free and paid Apple accounts work. A free account limits you to 3 sideloaded apps installed at a time. A paid membership (\$99/year) removes that limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I publish to the App Store from my phone?">
    Yes for Swift apps. Open More → Publish App, then use App Store Submissions for uploads, TestFlight, and beta invite links. Web apps are not published to the App Store from this flow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app won't launch after installing">
    Trust the developer on your iPhone: Settings → General → VPN and Device Management → tap your account → Trust. Also make sure Developer Mode is on: Settings → Privacy and Security → Developer Mode. On a free Apple Developer account, you may have hit the 3-app limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do updates work?">
    Rork Max updates itself over the air for most releases. Occasionally a major update requires reinstalling from rork.com/ios/install. That install always replaces the app on your phone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
