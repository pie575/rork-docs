App Store screenshots are one of the biggest drivers of installs. Most users decide whether to download an app based on the first few screenshots they see, so clarity, layout, and correct sizing are critical.

## Required screenshot sizes

Apple requires separate screenshots for iPhone and iPad if your app supports both.

### iPhone (Portrait)

Use the largest size, which Apple accepts for all smaller phones:

- **6.7-inch display (recommended):** 1290 × 2796 px, 9:16 aspect ratio

This size covers iPhone 14 Pro Max, 15 Pro Max, and newer large devices.

### iPad (Portrait)

If your app supports iPad, Apple requires iPad screenshots:

- **12.9-inch iPad:** 2048 × 2732 px, 4:3 aspect ratio

This size is accepted for all supported iPad models.

## Screenshot strategy

Before designing, plan your five slots:

- **Screenshot 1:** Core value proposition
- **Screenshots 2 to 3:** Main features
- **Screenshots 4 to 5:** Differentiation, results, or use cases

Keep text minimal and readable on small screens.

## Tools

**Canva (Recommended)**

1. Open Canva and search "App Store Screenshot"
2. Choose an iPhone (9:16) template or iPad (4:3) template
3. Add your app screenshots and text
4. Export as PNG

You can also create canvases manually using Apple's exact dimensions.

**Screenshots Pro:** A dedicated tool for App Store assets. Best for device frames, consistent layouts, and batch exporting.

**iOS Simulator:** Best for capturing raw screenshots directly. Use a 6.7-inch Pro Max device for iPhone, and a 12.9-inch iPad for iPad.

## Capturing screenshots

### iPhone

- Use the iOS Simulator in your Rork project
- Choose a **6.7-inch Pro Max** device
- Capture in portrait mode

### iPad

- Use iPad Simulator
- Choose **12.9-inch iPad**
- Capture in portrait unless your app is landscape-only

## Export guidelines

- Format: **PNG**
- No compression
- Keep text within safe margins
- High contrast and readable fonts

## Common mistakes

- Wrong dimensions or aspect ratio
- Missing iPad screenshots when iPad is supported
- Text too small to read on small screens
- Overcrowded designs with too much information

## Summary

Apple requires:

- **iPhone screenshots:** 1290 × 2796 px (9:16)
- **iPad screenshots:** 2048 × 2732 px (4:3)

Capture clean UI, design with Canva or Screenshots Pro, and upload both iPhone and iPad screenshots to ensure App Store compliance and higher conversion.
