Rork Cloud is a backend that Rork creates and hosts for your app, so you do not have to set one up or configure it yourself. When your app needs to store data, the agent shows a **Create Rork Cloud** confirmation in the chat. After you confirm, Rork provisions the backend in the background and connects it to your app automatically.

<Note>
  Rork Cloud is currently in **beta**. It is free to use while in beta, and your data is never deleted, even if your subscription ends.
</Note>

## What you get

- **No extra sign-ups.** Your Rork account is all you need. Rork manages everything for you, and you can ask the agent in chat for anything at any time.
- A **Postgres database** for accounts, shared data, and anything that must sync across devices and users.
- **Row Level Security** so each user can only read and write their own data.
- **Edge Functions** for logic that should not run on the user's device, such as private API keys, payments, and webhooks.

## Rork Cloud or your own Supabase?

Both work with Rork the same way, so pick based on what you want to own:

- **Never built a backend before?** Use Rork Cloud. Rork creates and hosts it for you, and there is nothing to set up or pay for while it is in beta.
- **Already comfortable with Supabase?** Connect your own Supabase project and do everything there, under your own account. Supabase bills you directly on [their pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing).

You are not locked in either way: you can start on Rork Cloud and [transfer to your own Supabase later](#transfer-to-your-own-supabase).

## How to add it

There are two ways to get a backend:

- **Just ask in chat.** Say "Add a database to my app" or "Add a backend" and Rork takes care of the rest.
- **From the project panel.** Click **More**, open **Database**, and press **Add a database**.

When Rork needs to create a backend, it shows a **Create Rork Cloud** confirmation card in the chat. Press **Create Rork Cloud** to confirm, or **Connect my Supabase** to bring your own project instead.

## See what your app is storing

Once your backend exists, the **Database** tab under **More** shows your tables and row counts.

If you want a friendlier view than raw tables, ask Rork in chat to build one:

```text
Build me a web admin panel where I can browse and edit my app's users and orders.
```

## Transfer to your own Supabase

If you later want the backend to belong to you and be managed by you, press **Transfer to your Supabase** at the top of the **Database** tab. The whole backend moves into your Supabase organization with your data. The project's URL, data, and keys stay the same, so your published app keeps working with zero changes.

Two things to know:

- The transfer is one-way. Rork cannot take the project back afterwards.
- Billing moves to you. From then on you pay Supabase directly for this project.

## What it costs

**Rork Cloud is free while it is in beta.** The database, access rules, and server-side functions do not cost anything to run right now.

Two related costs are separate from the backend itself:

- **AI features inside your app** (in-app chat, image generation, voice) run on **Cloud credits**, pay as you go. See [Build credits vs Cloud credits](/billing/build-credits-vs-cloud-credits).
- **After a transfer to your own Supabase**, Supabase bills you directly for the project.

## If you cancel or stop paying

- **Your data is not deleted.** Rork does not delete your app's data, even if your subscription ends.
- **Your app keeps working.** If you cancel your Rork subscription, your app and its backend keep running.

## When you may not need it

- Prototypes that only store data on one device can use local storage.
- Logic, webhooks, or private keys can use server-side functions without a full database.
