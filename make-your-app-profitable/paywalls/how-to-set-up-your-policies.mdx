Every app must include Terms and Conditions, a Privacy Policy, and an EULA to be approved on the App Store and to protect you legally. This guide explains what each one is and the fastest way to create it.

## End User License Agreement (EULA)

You do not need to write your own EULA. Apple allows you to use **Apple's Standard EULA**, which is accepted by default on the App Store.

When submitting your app, simply select **Apple's Standard EULA** in App Store Connect unless you have custom legal requirements.

## Privacy Policy

A Privacy Policy is required for all apps, especially if you collect user data, analytics, payment information, or account details.

Generate a compliant Privacy Policy with one of these tools:

- [**Termly**](https://termly.io) (recommended)
- [**PrivacyPolicies.com**](https://privacypolicies.com)
- [**Iubenda**](https://iubenda.com)

These tools let you answer a few questions about your app, generate a policy, and host it at a URL you can link to.

## Terms and Conditions

Terms and Conditions define how users are allowed to use your app and limit your liability. You can generate them with the same tools as above. Most tools bundle Terms and Privacy Policy together in one flow.

## Where to use these policies

Once you have URLs for each policy:

- Add them in your **App Store Connect listing** (Privacy Policy URL field)
- Link to them inside the app's **Settings screen** and on your **paywall**
- Include them in your App Store description to avoid rejection

<Tip>
  If you are hard-paywalling your app (all features require a subscription), mention this clearly in your App Store description. Apple will reject apps where this is hidden.
</Tip>
