---

Free trials are one of the most effective ways to convert users to paid subscribers. This guide shows how to add a free trial offer to an existing iOS subscription.

**Requirements:**

- RevenueCat already integrated. See [Add a paywall with RevenueCat](/payment-guides/revenuecat-copied-1).
- Subscriptions already configured in App Store Connect.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscriptions tab in App Store Connect">
    Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com), open your app, and go to **Subscriptions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click on the subscription to edit">
    Select the subscription you want to add a free trial to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the plus button to add an offer">
    Press **\+** to add a new introductory offer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Introductory offer and set the duration">
    Click **Introductory offer**, then set the duration (for example, 7 days or 1 month), set the price to \$0.00, and choose **Free trial** as the type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next and confirm">
    Review the details and save. Within about 20 minutes, your app should display the free trial offer on its paywall.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Free trials only appear to users who have never purchased that subscription before. Make sure your paywall shows the trial prominently so users know it is available.
</Tip>
