Screenshot Studio turns raw simulator screenshots into professional App Store artwork, at rork.com/screenshots. It is free while in beta and does not require an account. Your output is a complete deck of up to six slides, each with a headline, background art, and your screen framed inside an iPhone.

## How to generate screenshots

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give it a source">
    Provide an App Store URL (best, since Rork reads the name, description, and existing screenshots), a GitHub repo URL (useful for unpublished apps), or skip ahead and upload screens manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the app details">
    Rork fills in app name, category, and summary. Correct anything that is out of date, because the headline text is written from this summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick up to six screens">
    Choose the screens that carry your app's story. Use **Upload your own** for screens Rork could not find automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a look, or describe one">
    Layout and Style are optional. Pick a preset (dark premium, gradient dreamy, and more) or type your own direction in a sentence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate, then download">
    Generation takes about a minute. Use **Download all** to get the full deck, or click **Retry** on any individual slide you want to refine.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- PNG files at **1284 x 2778**, the iPhone 6.5-inch size that App Store Connect accepts for every modern iPhone
- A written headline per slide
- Background art per slide, tied together by a single palette
- Your screen placed inside an iPhone device frame

<Note>
  Output is sized for the App Store. For Google Play, generate Play listing images separately, because Play requires different asset sizes.
</Note>

## Write better headlines

Two habits will lift your results:

1. **Fix the summary before generating.** One clear sentence about who the app is for will beat a wall of keywords every time.
2. **Order screens like a pitch.** Lead with the feature people came for, and leave settings or profile screens for later.
