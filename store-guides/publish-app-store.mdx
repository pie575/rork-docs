Publishing an iPhone app through Rork is straightforward. Once you have your Apple Developer account, you enter a few details about your app and Rork handles the build and upload. No Xcode or Mac required.

You need an Apple Developer account before you begin. See [Create your Apple Developer account](/store-guides/apple-developer-account).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update your icon, app name, and bundle identifier">
    Inside your project, click on the title at the top left. This opens **Project Settings**.

    Update your **Project Name**, **bundle identifier**, and **App Icon** before submitting. Once finished, save your changes.

    <Tip>
      You can ask Rork in chat to generate an App Icon and it will integrate it automatically.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Publish to App Store">
    In your project, press **Publish → Publish to App Store**. A new window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your Apple Developer account">
    Rork builds the app on its own machines. You do not need Xcode, a Mac, or any other build account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start submission">
    Your project name and bundle ID are already filled in from the settings you updated earlier. Press **Start Submission**.

    <Warning>
      Stay on this page while the app builds and submits. At some point Apple will ask for a verification code on one of your signed-in Apple devices. Have that device handy to enter the code into Rork when asked.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check App Store Connect">
    When the app submits, it appears in your [App Store Connect Apps Dashboard](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After submission

From App Store Connect, complete your product page: screenshots, description, keywords, age rating, privacy information, and pricing. Then submit for review. Apple typically reviews apps within 24 to 48 hours.

Good luck! Tweet your app link to [@rork_app](https://x.com/rork_app).
