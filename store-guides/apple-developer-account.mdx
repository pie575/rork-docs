To publish apps to the App Store, you need an Apple Developer account. The enrollment takes a few minutes and costs \$99 per year. This guide walks you through each step.

> Join the Apple Developer Program to reach customers around the world on the App Store for all Apple platforms. Membership provides the tools, resources, and support you need to develop and distribute apps and games. — Apple Developer Program

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your enrollment">
    Go to [developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) and press **Start Your Enrollment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your Apple Account (or create one)">
    Use the same Apple Account you use on your iPhone, or create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Skip profile customization">
    After signing in, agree to the terms. On the developer profile customization screen, press **Remind Me Later** or **Not Interested**. It is not needed for most use cases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue enrollment on the web">
    Apple may suggest enrolling through the Apple Developer app. Look right below that for **Continue Enrollment on the Web** and press it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your personal information">
    Fill in your real name, address, and contact details accurately. Apple uses this information for payouts later, so any mismatch can cause problems.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your entity type">
    For most individual app makers, choose **Individual/Sole Proprietor** and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and accept the terms">
    Read through the final agreement and accept it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the yearly fee">
    Complete the purchase of your Apple Developer membership for \$99 per year. Prices may vary by country.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm your account is active

After enrollment finishes, log in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/). If you see the App Store Connect dashboard, your account is ready.

You can now publish iPhone apps from Rork directly to the App Store, or upload builds to TestFlight for beta testing.
