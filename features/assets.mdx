Rork generates the pictures, video, sound, and 3D models your app needs, keeps them in the Assets tab, and puts them into the code for you. Ask for them in chat, in plain words.

```text
Replace the placeholder photos with real product shots of dark roast coffee bags
```

## Where your media lives

Open **More → Assets** in a project. Everything Rork generated, and everything you attached to the chat, is collected there.

The filters are **Images**, **3D Models**, **Videos**, **Audio**, and **Other** for files like PDFs. Each card offers:

- **Use in Chat**, which attaches that asset to your next message
- **Remove**, which deletes it from the project
- The card itself, which opens the asset full size

<Tip>
  **Use in Chat** is the shortest way to say "this one". Attach the asset, then write "use this photo in the hero instead".
</Tip>

## Images

Ask in chat and Rork generates the image, saves it to Assets, and puts it in the screen you meant. Rork generates in three shapes: square, portrait, and landscape.

```text
Generate a landscape hero photo of a fire-lit roasting drum, dark and smoky
```

Images work the other way too. Attach a photo and ask Rork to build a feature around generating from it, which is how photo filter apps, avatar apps, and "turn my selfie into X" apps get made.

## Your app icon

The app icon is generated the same way:

```text
Generate an app icon: an ember on a dark background, no text
```

Rork writes it into the right place for each platform, so an iPhone build, an Android build, and a web app all pick it up.

## Video

Ask for video and Rork generates it, then places it in the screen:

```text
Add a looping background video of coffee beans falling into the roaster
```

Video generation can start from your text, from a still image, from a reference clip, or edit a video it already made. Video is the most expensive kind of media to generate, so describe the shot you want clearly before asking for it.

## Audio and voice

Rork generates four kinds of sound:

| Kind | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Music** | Background loops, menu music in a game |
| **Sound effects** | Taps, wins, notifications, whooshes |
| **Voice** | A narrator, an onboarding voice, an audio guide |
| **Dialogue** | Two or more speakers in a scene |

```text
Add a short success sound when the user finishes a workout
```

## 3D models

Rork generates 3D models for games, product viewers, and playful onboarding. It asks before it spends the credits, showing a card with what it plans to make and the cost:

- **Yes, make the 3D model** starts the work
- **No, and tell Rork what to do differently** lets you correct the idea first

```text
Add a spinning 3D coffee bag on the product page that users can drag to rotate
```

## Bring your own files

You do not have to generate everything. Use **\+ → Upload image** in the composer, or drop files onto the chat. Rork reads photos, screenshots, PDFs, video, audio, and 3D models, and anything you upload lands in Assets.

Free accounts upload files up to **10MB**. Paid plans go up to **5GB**, which is what video needs.

## What it costs

Generating media spends **build credits**, the same balance as your normal messages. Two habits keep the bill sensible:

1. Generate at the end, once the layout is settled. Placeholder boxes are free to move around.
2. Reuse from Assets with **Use in Chat** instead of generating a near-duplicate.

<Note>
  This page is about media Rork generates for you while you build. If you want your users to generate images or audio inside your finished app, that is a Rork Cloud feature. See [What is Rork Cloud](/backend/rork-cloud).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the generated images commercially?">
    Yes, they are assets of your app. Do not ask for a real brand's logo, a real person's face, or copyrighted artwork, because the App Store rejects apps over exactly that.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The image is nearly right. Can I fix it instead of starting again?">
    Yes. Attach it with **Use in Chat** and describe the change: "same photo, but brighter and without the mug on the left".
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upload my own brand photography?">
    Yes. Drop the files into the chat, then tell Rork where each one goes. Uploaded files sit in Assets next to the generated ones.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my Assets tab empty?">
    Nothing has been generated or attached yet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I download an asset?">
    Yes, open the card in the Assets tab. Everything is also in your repository if you connect GitHub.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
