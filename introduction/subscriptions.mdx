Rork has simple, transparent pricing. There is a **Free** plan and two paid tiers: **Rork Pro** and **Rork Max**. You only pay for the compute you use, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

<Note>
  Paid plans (Rork Pro and Rork Max) reset on the monthly anniversary of your first purchase. The Free plan resets at the start of each calendar month.
</Note>

## Design mode vs Build mode

Rork splits the work into two modes:

- **Design mode** explores app designs. You describe your idea and Rork proposes the screens and look. The Free plan runs in Design mode.
- **Build mode** turns those designs into a working app: real Swift, Kotlin, or React code that you can preview, test on a device, and publish. A paid plan unlocks Build mode.

## What is a credit?

A credit is a unit of AI compute, roughly one request where Rork designs, builds, or edits your app. Every plan includes a set number of credits per month. When you run out, you can upgrade your plan or wait for your monthly reset.

- **Free plan:** 5 design credits per day. 35 credits/month in major markets (US, UK, Canada, EU, Japan, Korea, Australia); 5 credits/month elsewhere.
- **Paid plans:** No daily cap, so you can spend your full monthly allowance whenever you want.

Some actions cost more than one credit. iOS builds and cloud simulator sessions rent real machines and are charged on top of the message that started them. See [Build credits vs Cloud credits](/billing/build-credits-vs-cloud-credits).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Rork Max to build an iPhone app?">
    No. Rork Pro builds native iPhone apps with SwiftUI. Rork Max is for the other Apple devices (iPad, Apple Watch, Vision Pro), native games, and iOS widgets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are my projects public on the Free plan?">
    No. Every project is private, on every plan, including Free.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do my credits reset?">
    Paid plans reset every month on the anniversary of your first purchase. The Free plan resets at the start of each calendar month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade my subscription?">
    On desktop, click your profile in the top right. If you are on the Free plan, click **Upgrade Plan**. If you already have a plan, click **Manage Subscription**. The full set of plans is available on desktop. In the mobile app, manage your subscription through the App Store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I downgrade or cancel?">
    On desktop, click your profile, then **Manage Subscription**, and choose a lower plan or cancel. On mobile, manage through the App Store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get free credits?">
    Yes. Connect your RevenueCat account at [rork.com/revenuecat-gift](https://rork.com/revenuecat-gift) to earn up to 1,000 free credits based on your app revenue. Active members of the [Rork Discord](https://discord.gg/rxBHytVKaT) can also earn credits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
