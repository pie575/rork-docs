title: "Publish your app"
---

Where your finished app can go: the App Store, Google Play, or the web. Each store has its own account, its own review, and its own waiting time. Start here and pick your route.

Rork builds and uploads for you, on its own machines. You do not need a Mac, Xcode, or Android Studio.

What Rork cannot do is be you. Each store wants an account in your name, your identity verified, and your listing written. Start those early, because the waiting is the slow part, not the building.

## Pick your route

<CardGroup>
  <Card icon="globe" href="/web/rork-web" title="The web">
    Fastest. No account, no review, no install. Publish and share a link today.
  </Card>

  <Card icon="apple" href="/store-guides/publish-app-store" title="App Store">
    An Apple Developer membership at \$99 a year, and Apple's review.
  </Card>

  <Card icon="android" href="/submitting-to-play-store/creating-the-aab-file" title="Google Play">
    A \$25 one-time registration, and a closed test for new personal accounts.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The three are not exclusive. One project can hold an iPhone app, an Android app, and a web app, and many founders publish the web app first to test the idea while the store accounts are being verified. See [Convert your app between iOS, Android, and web](/import/convert-between-platforms).

## iPhone, in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Apple Developer account">
    \$99 a year, and Apple verifies who you are. [Create your Apple Developer Account](/store-guides/apple-developer-account).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a build to TestFlight">
    Put the app on real phones, yours and your testers'. [TestFlight and testers](/store-guides/testflight-beta-testers).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check yourself against the guidelines">
    Run the [AI App Store Reviewer](/ai-app-store-reviewer) and read the [App Store Checklist](/submitting-to-app-store/apple/app-store-checklist).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Prepare the listing">
    Screenshots, description, privacy answers. [App Store screenshots](/appstore-screenshots/app-store-screenshots) and [Screenshot Studio](/store-guides/screenshot-studio).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit and wait">
    [Publish Your App to the Apple App Store](/store-guides/publish-app-store), then the [App Review guide](/appstore-submission-checklist/app-review-guide) for what the statuses mean.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a build fails or never appears, see [Submission errors](/submitting-to-app-store/apple/submission-errors). For the most common rejections, see [Main App Store rejection reasons](/appstore-submission-checklist/whats-next-after-the-apps-finished).

## Android, in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your Google Play developer account">
    \$25 once, plus identity verification. New personal accounts also need a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days, so start this first. [Create your Google Play developer account](/store-guides/google-play-developer-account).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the app on your own phone">
    [How to test Android apps on your Android device](/submitting-to-play-store/apk-file).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a build to Play Console">
    [How to publish your Rork app to Google Play Store](/submitting-to-play-store/creating-the-aab-file).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you submit anywhere

- **Test the thing you are shipping**, on a real device, not only in the preview.
- **Have your policies ready.** Both stores ask for a privacy policy, and a subscription needs terms. See [How to create a privacy policy](/make-your-app-profitable/paywalls/how-to-set-up-your-policies).
- **Decide how it makes money** before the listing, because pricing and paywalls affect review. See [How to add a paywall with RevenueCat](/payment-guides/revenuecat-copied-1).
