Android apps in Rork come with a built-in Android emulator that runs the app in your browser. When you want the app on your own phone, you have two ways: install the APK by hand (no Play account needed), or test through Google Play's internal testing track.

<Note>
  An iPhone (Swift) app is iOS only and does not run on Android. To reach Android users, add an Android app to the same project. See [Convert your app between platforms](/import/convert-between-platforms).
</Note>

## Install the APK on your own phone

Rork builds the APK for you, so you do not need a terminal or any build service:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Publish in your project">
    Click **Publish** at the top of the page, then open the Google Play section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download APK">
    If the button is not there yet, build the app first and wait for the build to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install on your Android phone">
    Open the downloaded `.apk` file on your Android phone and install it. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test through Google Play instead

Use Google Play internal testing when you want testers to install from the Play Store, or when you want to verify your Play Console setup before launch:

1. Follow the [Google Play publishing guide](/submitting-to-play-store/creating-the-aab-file) to build the AAB and send it to an internal testing track.
2. Add your own email as a tester and accept the invite link.
3. Install the app on your phone directly from the Play Store.

Internal testing releases are only visible to your testers. This path also verifies your Play Console setup, so it is the right choice before your real launch.
