Google Play requires an AAB (Android App Bundle) file for distribution. Rork builds the AAB for you and can upload it directly to your Play Console.

**Prerequisites:**

- A [Google Play Developer account](/store-guides/google-play-developer-account)
- A desktop or laptop (you cannot do this from a phone)

<Note>
  An iPhone (Swift) app cannot go to Google Play. To reach Android users, add an Android app to the same project. See [Convert your app between platforms](/import/convert-between-platforms).
</Note>

## Build the AAB in Rork

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Publish, then Google Play">
    In your project, click **Publish** at the top of the page, then open the Google Play section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google Play">
    Click **Connect Google Play** and sign in with the Google account that manages this Play Console app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build AAB for Play">
    Click **Build AAB for Play**. Rork builds a release AAB signed with a persistent upload key unique to your project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or download the AAB">
    When the build finishes, choose how to deliver it:

    - **Send to Google Play**: uploads the AAB to your internal testing track automatically. This appears once Rork verifies that your Google account can manage the Play Console app.
    - **Download AAB for Play Console**: downloads the signed `.aab` file so you can upload it by hand.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not ask Rork to add its own signing keys, keystores, or SHA1 fingerprints to the project. Google Play permanently associates your app with the key used for the first upload. Rork manages this key for you. If Play Console reports a signing key or SHA1 mismatch, contact support with your project link instead of regenerating anything.
</Warning>

## Upload the AAB in Play Console manually

If you downloaded the file, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the app in Play Console">
    Log in to [Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/developers) and click **Create App**. Fill in the app name, default language, whether it is an app or game, and whether it is free or paid.

    **Important:** If you intend for this to be a paid app, mark it as paid now. You can make a paid app free later, but you cannot make a free app paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Internal Testing">
    Select **View Tasks** under internal testing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add testers">
    Click **Select Testers**, scroll to the Testers tab, and click **Create email list**. Add your own email first so you can test the app yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new release">
    Click **Create a new release**, then upload the AAB file you downloaded from Rork.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in release details">
    Enter the release name and any release notes (such as the Rork version number you uploaded for reference), then click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    On the summary screen, click **Save and Publish**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invite link">
    Go back to the Internal Testing page and scroll down to the Testers tab. Copy the link that says **testers can join you on the web**. Send the link to yourself, accept the invitation, and install the app from the Play Store.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once internal testing looks good, promote the release to a broader track in Play Console when you are ready to launch.

For app updates, make your changes in Rork and click **Rebuild AAB**, then send the new build to Google Play or download and upload it as a new release in Play Console.
