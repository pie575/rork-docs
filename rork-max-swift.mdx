Rork Max is the plan for builders who need the full Apple device family. Where Rork Pro focuses on iPhone, Android, and web, Rork Max adds iPad, Apple Watch, Vision Pro, native games, and iOS widgets, all compiled to native Swift.

## What Rork Max adds

All Max projects include everything in Pro, plus:

- **iPad apps** with adaptive layouts
- **Apple Watch apps** (watchOS)
- **Vision Pro apps** (visionOS spatial experiences)
- **Native games** with native performance
- **iOS widgets** (home screen and lock screen)
- **Priority chat support**

## Same workflow, more platforms

Everything you know from Rork Pro works the same way. To add a new Apple platform to an existing project, click **\+** in the project header or ask in chat, just like adding Android.

## Pricing

Rork Max starts at **\$200/month** for 1,000 credits. See [Plans and Subscriptions](/introduction/subscriptions) for all tiers.

<Note>
  You do not need Rork Max to build iPhone apps. Rork Pro already includes native iPhone (SwiftUI) apps. Rork Max is only for the extended Apple device family.
</Note>
