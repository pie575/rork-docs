RevenueCat is the recommended way to add in-app purchases and subscriptions to your Rork app. This guide walks through the complete setup from connecting RevenueCat to testing real purchases on TestFlight.

A video walkthrough of this process is also available on the Rork YouTube channel.

## Step 1: Build your app in Rork

Write your prompt and let Rork build the app. If you think of additional features mid-build, add them to the queue so Rork can handle them after the first prompt.

## Step 2: Connect RevenueCat inside Rork

1. In Rork, go to **Integrations**.
2. Click **Connect with RevenueCat**.
3. Log in or sign up in RevenueCat.
4. Authorize Rork to access your RevenueCat account.
5. Click **Create new project** in RevenueCat and name it (for example, "Calorie Tracker").
6. Go back to the Rork chat and say: **"Set up RevenueCat."**

Rork will set up Offerings, Products, Entitlements, and environment variables. When Rork asks to set environment variables, click **Yes**.

## Step 3: Test purchases in the RevenueCat sandbox

1. In the app, tap the subscription (for example, \$40/year).
2. You will see options like "Test a valid purchase" and "Test failed purchase".
3. Tap **Test valid purchase**.
4. In the RevenueCat dashboard, go to **Overview** and toggle **Sandbox data**.

If you see sandbox MRR or the subscription amount, Rork and the RevenueCat Test Store are connected. Note: these are not real App Store subscriptions yet.

## Step 4: Push the app to TestFlight

Real Apple in-app purchases require a TestFlight build.

1. In Rork, go to **Publish → Submit to TestFlight**.
2. Click **New submission**.
3. (Optional) Generate an app icon using Rork.
4. Fill out Apple Developer information when prompted.
5. Submit and wait for the build and upload to complete.
6. In App Store Connect, complete the required compliance info so the build is testable.

## Step 5: Create subscriptions in App Store Connect

### Create a subscription group

1. In App Store Connect, go to **Subscriptions**.
2. Create a **Subscription Group** (name it something simple like "Subscriptions").

### Create monthly and yearly subscriptions

1. Click **Create Subscription** and set:
   - Reference name: "Monthly subscription"
   - Product ID: "monthly_subscription"
   - Duration: 1 month
   - Price: for example \$4.99/month
   - Add localization with a display name
   - Add a screenshot of your paywall (required for submission readiness)
2. Repeat the same steps for **Yearly**.

After saving, both products should be "Ready to submit" in App Store Connect.

## Step 6: Connect App Store Connect to RevenueCat

In RevenueCat, go to **Apps → iOS app** and fill in:

**Bundle ID:** Copy from App Store Connect.

**In-App Purchase Key:**

1. In App Store Connect, go to **Users and Access → Integrations → In-App Purchase** and click **\+**.
2. Name the key and download it.
3. Upload it to RevenueCat and paste the **Issuer ID**.

**App Store Connect API Key:**

1. Still in **Users and Access → Integrations**, create an **App Store Connect API** key.
2. Download and upload it to RevenueCat, then enter the **Issuer ID** again.

**Vendor Number:** Found in App Store Connect under **Reports** (top left of the page).

**App-Specific Shared Secret:**

1. In App Store Connect, go to **Apps → Your app → App Information**.
2. Scroll to **App-Specific Shared Secret**, click **Manage → Generate**, and copy the secret.
3. Paste it into RevenueCat and click **Save changes**.

## Step 7: Import your App Store products into RevenueCat

1. In RevenueCat, go to **Product Catalog**.
2. Click **Add new product → Import products**.
3. Select the monthly and yearly products and click **Import**.
4. For each imported product, attach an **Entitlement** (the same entitlement for both is common).

## Step 8: Attach products to your RevenueCat offering

1. In RevenueCat, go to **Offerings → Default**.
2. Click **Edit** and attach each App Store product to the correct package.
3. Click **Save**.

## Step 9: Final test on TestFlight

1. Install your app via TestFlight.
2. Open your paywall in-app.
3. Your subscriptions should now be purchasable via TestFlight and in production after release.

## Common issues

**Agreements and bank information not active:** Your subscriptions will not work if this is not set up in App Store Connect. Check under **Agreements, Tax, and Banking**.

**App Store public API key not set properly:** Copy the key and tell Rork: "Make sure Apple and RevenueCat are connected."

## What to do next

After adding a paywall, you will still be rejected from the App Store if you do not have a privacy policy, terms and conditions, and a visible EULA. In your App Store description, mention that every feature requires a paid subscription if you are hard-paywalling your app.

See [How to add app policies](/make-your-app-profitable/paywalls/how-to-set-up-your-policies).
